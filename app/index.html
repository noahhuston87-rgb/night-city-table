How does this work?
  1. The DM clicks Create new game. That browser tab is now the game server. Keep it open while you play. Everything is saved on the DM's computer, and the DM can have as many games as they like.
  2. The DM clicks Invite in the top bar and sends the link to the players.
  3. Players open the link, type their name, and they're in. No account and nothing to install.
  4. To bring in a character from cyberpunk2020character.com, open the Sheets tab and click Import.

Putting it online (one time, free): go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the whole night-city-table folder (the one with index.html inside) onto the page. You'll get a web address ending in netlify.app. Make a free Netlify account when it offers, so the site stays up for good. After that, everyone uses that address. Any DM can host their own games there, and each DM's games stay saved on their own computer.
